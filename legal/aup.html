Acceptable Use Policy

Product: offplan.online Version: 0.1 (draft) Effective: [DATE]

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to all users of the offplan.online platform, including Customers (subscribing businesses) and End Users. It forms part of the Terms of Service. Customers are responsible for ensuring their End Users comply with this AUP.

1.Permitted Use

The Service may be used solely for:

  • Marketing and presenting off-plan property developments to prospective buyers
  • Managing property listings, unit availability, and sales collateral
  • White-labelling the platform under your business brand to present to your clients
  • Integrating the Service with your CRM, website, or other authorised business tools via the API
  • Administrative and analytical activities related to your property sales operations

2.Prohibited Uses

The following are strictly prohibited. Violation may result in immediate suspension or termination.

2.1 Illegal and Harmful Content

  • Publishing false, misleading, or fraudulent property information (prices, availability, specifications, planning approvals)
  • Publishing Content that infringes third-party intellectual property rights (renders, images, plans without licence)
  • Money laundering, fraud, or use of the platform to facilitate illegal property transactions
  • Violating anti-money laundering (AML) or know-your-customer (KYC) obligations applicable to your jurisdiction
  • Publishing obscene, defamatory, or discriminatory content
  • Impersonating another business, developer, or person

2.2 Platform Integrity

  • Reverse engineering, decompiling, or disassembling any part of the Service
  • Scraping, crawling, or systematically extracting data from the Service by automated means beyond normal API use
  • Attempting to gain unauthorised access to other Customers' accounts, data, or infrastructure
  • Uploading malware, viruses, or any malicious code
  • Interfering with or disrupting the Service, servers, or networks connected to the Service
  • Bypassing or circumventing security controls, rate limits, or access restrictions
  • Using the Service to perform security testing against third-party systems without authorisation

2.3 API and Integration Misuse

  • Exceeding published API rate limits or making requests that unreasonably burden the infrastructure
  • Using the API to build a competing product or to extract data for resale
  • Sharing API credentials with unauthorised third parties
  • Using the API for purposes not related to the offplan.online Service

2.4 Resale and White-labelling Restrictions

  • Reselling, sublicensing, or white-labelling access to the Service without a valid Reseller Agreement
  • Allowing unaffiliated third parties to use your account credentials or session

3.Content Standards

  1. All property information published through the Service must be accurate to the best of your knowledge and clearly labelled where subject to change (e.g. indicative pricing, planning in progress).
  2. Renders, images, and walkthrough content must be licensed or owned by you. The Company accepts no liability for third-party IP claims arising from Customer Content.
  3. End User-facing content must comply with advertising standards applicable in the territory where the property is located (e.g. UAE RERA guidelines, AU Consumer Law advertising rules).
  4. Content must not discriminate against buyers on the basis of race, religion, nationality, gender, disability, or other protected characteristics.

5.Security Responsibilities

  1. You must not share account passwords or API keys in public repositories, support tickets, or any insecure channel.
  2. You are responsible for keeping your authentication credentials secure and rotating them if compromised.
  3. You must promptly report any suspected security vulnerabilities to security@offplan.online and not exploit or disclose them publicly before they are resolved.

6.Enforcement

  1. The Company monitors platform usage for AUP compliance and reserves the right to investigate suspected violations.
  2. For minor violations, the Company will typically provide notice and a reasonable opportunity to remedy before taking further action.
  3. For serious violations — including illegal activity, fraud, or wilful disruption — the Company may suspend or terminate access immediately without prior notice.
  4. Terminated accounts are not entitled to refunds for any unused portion of their subscription period.
  5. The Company may report illegal activity to relevant law enforcement or regulatory authorities.

7.Reporting Violations

To report suspected AUP violations by another Customer or End User, contact trust@offplan.online. Reports are treated confidentially.

8.Changes

The Company may update this AUP with 30 days' notice. Continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the updated AUP.
