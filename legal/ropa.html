Record of Processing Activities (ROPA)

Legal basis: GDPR Art. 30 Controller: offplan.online Ltd (Cyprus) Version: 0.1 (draft) Last reviewed: [DATE] Review frequency: Quarterly / on any material change

This Record of Processing Activities is maintained in compliance with GDPR Art. 30. As a data controller and as a data processor (on behalf of Customers), offplan.online Ltd must maintain this record and make it available to the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection on request. This document covers both roles.

Two Roles offplan.online acts as (A) a Data Controller for its own business data (account holders, marketing, HR) and as (B) a Data Processor for Customer data (End User personal data within Customer projects). Both are documented below.
