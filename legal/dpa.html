Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Product: offplan.online Version: 0.1 (draft) Regulation: GDPR (EU) 2016/679 Governing law: Cyprus

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between offplan.online Ltd ("Processor") and the subscribing Customer ("Controller"). It governs the processing of personal data by the Processor on behalf of the Controller in the course of providing the Service.

Relationship The Customer (Controller) determines the purposes and means of processing End User personal data within their Projects. offplan.online (Processor) processes that data only as instructed by the Controller. This DPA is required by Art. 28 GDPR and applies automatically upon subscription.

1.Definitions

Terms defined in the Terms of Service have the same meaning here. Additionally:

  • "Controller" — the Customer, who determines the purposes and means of processing.
  • "Processor" — offplan.online Ltd, processing data on the Controller's behalf.
  • "Personal Data" — any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • "Data Subject" — the individual to whom Personal Data relates (typically an End User).
  • "Processing" — any operation performed on Personal Data.
  • "Sub-processor" — a third party engaged by the Processor to process Personal Data.
  • "GDPR" — Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council.
  • "Supervisory Authority" — the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection, or any other competent authority.

2.Processing Instructions

  1. The Processor shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller, as set out in Annex A to this DPA, unless required to do so by EU or Member State law.
  2. The Processor shall immediately inform the Controller if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes GDPR or other applicable data protection law.
  3. The Controller warrants that it has a valid legal basis for all processing instructed under this DPA.

3.Confidentiality

The Processor shall ensure that persons authorised to process Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

4.Security (Art. 32 GDPR)

The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including as set out in Annex B. These measures include:

  • Encryption of Personal Data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Access controls and principle of least privilege
  • Regular security testing and vulnerability assessments
  • Logical separation of Customer data between tenants
  • Audit logging of access to personal data
  • Employee training on data protection obligations

5.Sub-processors (Art. 28(2) GDPR)

  1. The Controller provides general authorisation for the Processor to engage sub-processors, subject to the conditions in this section.
  2. The Processor's current sub-processors are listed in Annex C. The Processor shall notify the Controller of any intended changes (additions or replacements) at least 30 days in advance.
  3. The Controller may object to a new sub-processor within 14 days of notification. If the parties cannot resolve the objection, either party may terminate the affected service with 30 days' notice.
  4. The Processor shall impose data protection obligations on all sub-processors equivalent to those in this DPA, by way of written contract.
  5. The Processor remains fully liable to the Controller for the performance of sub-processors.

6.Data Subject Rights

The Processor shall:

  1. Notify the Controller without undue delay of any Data Subject request received directly by the Processor relating to the Controller's data.
  2. Provide reasonable assistance to the Controller in responding to Data Subject rights requests (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) within the timeframes required by GDPR.
  3. Not respond directly to Data Subject requests on the Controller's behalf unless expressly authorised to do so.

7.Personal Data Breach Notification (Art. 33–34 GDPR)

  1. The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay — and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware — of any Personal Data Breach affecting the Controller's data.
  2. The notification shall include: (a) nature and categories of data affected; (b) approximate number of data subjects and records; (c) likely consequences; (d) measures taken or proposed.
  3. The Controller is responsible for notifying the Supervisory Authority and affected Data Subjects in accordance with GDPR Art. 33–34.

8.Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)

The Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to the Controller in carrying out DPIAs and, where required, prior consultations with the Supervisory Authority, as contemplated by Art. 35–36 GDPR.

9.Deletion and Return of Data

  1. On termination of the Terms of Service, the Processor shall make Customer Data available for export for 30 days.
  2. After the 30-day export window, the Processor shall delete all Personal Data processed on behalf of the Controller, unless retention is required by applicable law.
  3. On request, the Processor shall provide written certification of deletion.

10.Audit Rights (Art. 28(3)(h) GDPR)

  1. The Processor shall make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and shall allow for and contribute to audits conducted by the Controller or its mandated auditor.
  2. Audits require at least 30 days' prior written notice and shall not unreasonably disrupt the Processor's operations. The costs of an audit are borne by the Controller unless the audit reveals a material breach.
  3. The Processor may satisfy audit requests by providing current third-party certifications (e.g. ISO 27001, SOC 2) or penetration test reports in lieu of direct audit access.

11.International Transfers

  1. The Processor shall not transfer Personal Data outside the EEA without the Controller's authorisation.
  2. Authorised transfers to sub-processors outside the EEA are covered by: (a) EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs, Commission Decision 2021/914); or (b) an adequacy decision; or (c) another valid transfer mechanism.

12.Liability and Indemnification

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations set out in the Terms of Service. Where a party has paid compensation to a Data Subject or supervisory authority for a breach caused by the other party, the paying party is entitled to recover that portion from the responsible party.

13.Term

This DPA is effective from the date of the Terms of Service and terminates on their expiry or termination, subject to the data deletion obligations in §9 which survive termination.

Annex B — Technical & Organisational Measures

  • Encryption in transit: TLS 1.2 minimum, TLS 1.3 preferred
  • Encryption at rest: AES-256 for stored data and backups
  • Access control: role-based, principle of least privilege, MFA enforced for admin access
  • Audit logging: all access to personal data logged with user, timestamp, and action
  • Network security: WAF, DDoS protection (Cloudflare), VPC isolation
  • Vulnerability management: automated dependency scanning, quarterly penetration testing
  • Incident response: documented procedure, 72-hour notification commitment
  • Employee training: annual data protection training; confidentiality obligations in employment contracts
  • Physical security: hosted on SOC 2-certified cloud infrastructure (AWS)
  • Backups: daily encrypted backups, 30-day retention, tested quarterly
