1.Definitions

  • "Uptime" — the percentage of minutes in a calendar month during which the Service is Available.
  • "Available" — the Service is accessible and functional for the primary use case (Sales App display and Admin Panel access). Degraded performance of non-core features does not constitute Unavailability.
  • "Downtime" — any period during which the Service is Unavailable, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and Excluded Events.
  • "Scheduled Maintenance" — planned maintenance notified at least 48 hours in advance, conducted during low-traffic windows.
  • "Incident" — an unplanned event causing or risking Downtime or significant degradation.
  • "SLA Credit" — a credit applied to future invoices, as defined in §4.

3.Excluded Events

The following are excluded from Downtime calculations:

  • Scheduled Maintenance (notified ≥ 48h in advance)
  • Events caused by Customer or End User actions (misconfiguration, exceeding API limits, invalid content)
  • Force majeure events (natural disasters, war, civil unrest, power grid failure)
  • Failures of third-party services outside the Company's reasonable control (DNS providers, ISPs, CDN providers not under contract)
  • DDoS attacks exceeding the Company's mitigation capacity
  • Beta or preview features explicitly labelled as such

6.Incident Communication

  1. Service status is published at status.offplan.online.
  2. For P1 Incidents, the Company will post an initial status update within 30 minutes of detection.
  3. Progress updates will be posted at intervals of ≤ 1 hour until resolution.
  4. A post-incident report (PIR) will be published within 5 business days of resolution for P1 Incidents affecting multiple Customers.

7.Planned Maintenance

  1. Scheduled Maintenance will be notified at least 48 hours in advance via email and the status page.
  2. Maintenance will preferably be conducted between 02:00 and 06:00 CET on weekdays.
  3. The Company targets ≤ 4 hours of Scheduled Maintenance per calendar month.
  4. Emergency maintenance (for critical security patches) may be performed with shorter notice; the Company will notify as promptly as practical.

8.Data Backup and Recovery

  • Backup frequency: Daily automated backups
  • Backup retention: 30 days
  • Recovery Point Objective (RPO): ≤ 24 hours (data loss in worst case)
  • Recovery Time Objective (RTO): ≤ 4 hours (time to restore from backup)
  • Backups are encrypted and stored in a geographically separate location within the EU

9.Measurement

Uptime is measured by the Company's monitoring systems (synthetic probes from multiple regions). The Company's measurements are authoritative for SLA Credit calculations. Customers may provide independent monitoring data as supporting evidence in a Credit claim.

10.SLA Review

The Company may update this SLA with 60 days' notice. Changes will not reduce commitments for the current billing period.
