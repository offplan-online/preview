MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Effective Date: DD Month YYYY

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date above between:

Party A: offplan.online Ltd, a company incorporated in Cyprus (company number [REG NO]), whose registered office is at [ADDRESS], Cyprus ("offplan.online"); and

Party B: [FULL LEGAL NAME], a [company / partnership / sole trader] incorporated/registered in [COUNTRY] (registration number [REG NO IF APPLICABLE]), whose address is [ADDRESS] ("[SHORT NAME]").

The parties are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

Purpose: The Parties wish to explore a potential [partnership / integration / reseller arrangement / investment / other] (the "Proposed Transaction") and may disclose Confidential Information to each other in the course of these discussions.

1.Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party"), whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by any other means, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes, without limitation:

  • Business plans, financial data, forecasts, and pricing
  • Technical specifications, source code, algorithms, and product roadmaps
  • Customer lists, supplier relationships, and contractual arrangements
  • Marketing strategies and business methodologies
  • Any information relating to the Proposed Transaction or these discussions

2.Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

  1. Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party;
  2. Was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, as evidenced by written records;
  3. Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Confidential Information; or
  4. Is received from a third party without restriction and without breach of any confidentiality obligation.

3.Obligations

  1. The Receiving Party shall: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not disclose it to any third party without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent; (c) use it solely for the purpose of evaluating the Proposed Transaction; and (d) protect it with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and in any event not less than reasonable care.
  2. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to its directors, officers, employees, and professional advisers ("Authorised Recipients") who: (a) have a need to know for the purpose of evaluating the Proposed Transaction; and (b) are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement.
  3. The Receiving Party is liable for any breach of this Agreement by its Authorised Recipients.

4.Compelled Disclosure

If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it shall: (a) provide prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party (to the extent permitted by law); (b) cooperate with the Disclosing Party's efforts to seek a protective order; and (c) disclose only the minimum amount required.

5.No Licence

Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any licence, right, or interest in any intellectual property of the other Party. No licence is implied by the disclosure of Confidential Information.

6.Return or Destruction

On request by the Disclosing Party, or upon termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all materials (including copies, notes, and extracts) containing Confidential Information and, on request, certify in writing that it has done so. Electronic records that cannot reasonably be deleted (e.g. email backup systems) remain subject to ongoing confidentiality obligations.

7.Term

This Agreement is effective from the Effective Date for a period of 2 years, unless earlier terminated by either Party on 30 days' written notice. Obligations with respect to Confidential Information disclosed during the term survive termination for a further 3 years.

8.Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, each Party is entitled to seek equitable relief, including injunction, in addition to all other remedies available at law.

9.General

  1. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Cyprus. Both Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Cyprus, except that either Party may seek emergency equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.
  2. Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions and agreements.
  3. Amendment. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
  4. No Waiver. Failure to enforce any provision does not constitute a waiver.
  5. Severability. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder continues in full force.
  6. Counterparts. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including electronic counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
