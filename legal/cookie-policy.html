1.What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognise your device, remember your preferences, and collect usage data. Similar technologies include:

  • Local storage / session storage — browser-side storage used for session data
  • Pixel tags / web beacons — tiny images that track page visits or email opens
  • Fingerprinting — we do not use device fingerprinting

4.Cookie Banner Implementation

The cookie consent banner is displayed on first visit and provides clear accept/reject choices per category. The implementation must:

  • Present "Accept all", "Reject non-essential", and "Customise" options at equal prominence (no dark patterns)
  • Not set non-essential cookies before consent is given
  • Allow users to withdraw consent at any time with equal ease to giving it
  • Log consent with timestamp and version for audit purposes
  • Re-prompt after 12 months or when the cookie list materially changes
Implementation Note Recommended tools: iubenda, Cookiebot, or CookieYes. Do not build a custom banner — the compliance requirements (pre-loading prevention, consent logging, IAB TCF where needed) make custom builds error-prone. Budget ~€30-60/month. See Legal Sources → Templated Tools.

5.Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can manage cookies in three ways:

  1. Cookie Settings button — in the footer of every page. Opens the preference panel to change category consents.
  2. Browser settings — most browsers allow you to view, delete, and block cookies. Note: blocking essential cookies will prevent access to the Admin Panel. See guidance:
    • Chrome
    • Firefox
    • Safari
  3. Opt-out tools — for analytics cookies, you may use browser extensions such as uBlock Origin.

6.End-User Deployments (White-Label)

The offplan.online platform is also deployed under Customer brands (white-label). In those deployments:

  • The Customer (e.g. the rendering studio or agency) is responsible for their own cookie consent implementation
  • The Customer's cookie policy governs cookies on their deployment
  • The End-User Notice template at offplan.online/legal/end-user-notice provides guidance for Customers on what to disclose
  • offplan.online's own cookies (session management, security) will still be present and are covered by this policy and by the Customer's DPA with offplan.online

7.Legal Basis

Cookie use is governed by the EU ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC, as amended) and GDPR. The legal basis for:

  • Essential cookies: strictly necessary for the service you requested — no consent required (Art. 5(3) ePrivacy)
  • All other cookies: consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) — given via the cookie banner

For Australian users, cookie use is also covered by the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (APP 3 — collection). For UAE users, the UAE PDPL (Decree-Law No. 45/2021) applies.
