Reseller Agreement

Product: offplan.online Version: 0.1 (draft) Governing law: Cyprus

This Reseller Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between offplan.online Ltd ("Company") and the undersigned reseller ("Reseller"). It governs the Reseller's right to white-label and resell the offplan.online platform to its clients under its own brand.

What This Agreement Covers Rendering studios, agencies, and brokerages ("Resellers") who wish to offer the offplan.online platform to their property developer clients under their own subdomain and branding. The Reseller maintains the client relationship; the Company provides the underlying infrastructure.

1.Appointment

  1. The Company appoints the Reseller as a non-exclusive reseller of the Service within the territory specified in the Order Form ("Territory").
  2. The Reseller may present and sell the Service to its clients under the Reseller's own brand via a white-label deployment, subject to the conditions of this Agreement.
  3. The Reseller is not authorised to appoint sub-resellers without the Company's prior written consent.

2.White-Label Rights

  1. The Company grants the Reseller a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to present the Service under the Reseller's brand name, logo, and custom domain, subject to Brand Guidelines in §6.
  2. The Reseller may customise the colour scheme and logo of the Sales App and Admin Panel within the configuration options provided.
  3. The Reseller must not remove, obscure, or misrepresent the underlying technical origin of the Service in any way that would mislead End Users about the nature of the product.
  4. "Powered by offplan.online" attribution in the platform footer is required unless a specific exemption is agreed in writing with the Company.

3.Reseller Pricing and Revenue

  1. The Reseller pays the Company the wholesale price set out in the Order Form for each client seat or project provisioned.
  2. The Reseller may charge its clients any price it determines, provided it is not below the Minimum Resale Price (if any) specified in the Order Form.
  3. The Reseller is responsible for all invoicing, collection, and tax obligations with respect to its clients. The Company invoices only the Reseller.
  4. Revenue sharing arrangements (if applicable) are specified in the Order Form and are calculated and paid quarterly.

4.Reseller Obligations

  1. The Reseller shall:
    • Enter into written terms with each client that are at least as protective of the Company and the Service as these Terms and the Company's standard ToS
    • Include data processing provisions equivalent to the Company's DPA in each client contract
    • Ensure clients comply with the AUP
    • Promptly notify the Company of any client misconduct or AUP violation
    • Provide first-line support to its clients; escalate to the Company only for technical issues beyond the Reseller's control
    • Not make any representation, warranty, or guarantee about the Service beyond what is set out in the SLA, without the Company's prior written consent
  2. The Reseller is responsible for its clients' compliance with applicable laws, including property advertising regulations, AML/KYC obligations, and data protection requirements in the relevant jurisdiction.

5.Client Relationship

  1. The client relationship is owned by the Reseller. The Company does not communicate directly with the Reseller's clients except where required by law or in a security emergency.
  2. If the Reseller fails to pay the Company, the Company may suspend access to all client projects provisioned under the Reseller's account, with 7 days' notice to the Reseller.
  3. If this Agreement terminates, the Company will offer the Reseller's clients the option to migrate to direct Company accounts at the Company's then-current pricing. The Reseller must notify its clients of the termination.

6.Brand Guidelines

  1. The Reseller must not use the offplan.online name, logo, or trademarks in external communications without prior written approval, except as permitted by these guidelines.
  2. The Reseller may state that their platform is "built on offplan.online technology" in written agreements or RFP responses.
  3. The Reseller must not position the Service as their own proprietary technology in any investor presentation, funding document, or M&A context without disclosure of the underlying licence.

7.Intellectual Property

No intellectual property in the Service is transferred to the Reseller. The Reseller acquires only the limited licence rights expressly granted in this Agreement. All client-submitted Content remains the property of the respective client or Reseller as applicable.

8.Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential: pricing terms, technical architecture details, client lists, and any other information designated as confidential. This obligation survives termination for 3 years.

9.Term and Termination

  1. This Agreement commences on the date of execution and continues for 12 months, renewing automatically on the same terms unless either party gives 60 days' written notice of non-renewal.
  2. Either party may terminate immediately for material breach if the breach is not remedied within 21 days of written notice.
  3. The Company may terminate immediately if the Reseller: (a) becomes insolvent; (b) violates applicable laws; or (c) acts in a way that materially harms the Company's reputation.
  4. Termination does not affect accrued payment obligations or clauses intended to survive (confidentiality, indemnification, IP).

10.Liability

  1. The Company's liability to the Reseller is capped at the fees paid by the Reseller in the 12 months prior to the claim.
  2. The Reseller indemnifies the Company against any claims arising from the Reseller's clients' use of the Service or the Reseller's acts or omissions.

11.Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Cyprus. Disputes shall be referred to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Cyprus.
