End-User Notice

Purpose: Transparency notice for property buyers using a white-label deployment Version: 0.1 (draft)
How to use this document This notice is displayed to End Users (property buyers, agents) who access a white-label deployment of the offplan.online platform. It is typically rendered as a footer link ("Privacy Notice" or "About this platform") or shown on first visit. The deploying Customer (e.g. the rendering studio or agency) fills in the highlighted fields for their brand. Two versions are provided: a short-form for inline display and a full-form for a dedicated page.

Version A — Short Form (Footer / Cookie Banner)

Suitable for a footer link, cookie notice, or brief inline notice. ~100 words.

This property presentation platform is operated by [STUDIO/AGENCY NAME] and is powered by offplan.online technology. When you use this platform, certain data (such as your browsing activity and any enquiry details you submit) is processed by both [STUDIO/AGENCY NAME] and offplan.online Ltd (Cyprus) to operate the service.

For information about how your data is used and your rights, see [STUDIO/AGENCY NAME]'s Privacy Policy and offplan.online's Privacy Policy.

To exercise your data rights or ask a question, contact: [STUDIO EMAIL]

Version B — Full Form (Dedicated Page)

Suitable for a dedicated "About this platform" or "Privacy Notice" page linked from the footer.

Property purchases involve significant financial decisions — seek independent legal and financial advice before committing.
